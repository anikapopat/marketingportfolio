South Asian Student Association

The South Asian Student Association at Suffolk University brings awareness to South Asian culture and builds a community for students who identify with the culture or want to support it. I worked on its brand repositioning, weekly content, and event marketing.

Objectives

  • Establish a recognizable brand identity
  • Grow digital presence
  • Strengthen community engagement
  • Drive event and general meeting awareness and attendance
  • Position SASA as a leading student organization
02

Brand Repositioning

SASA’s posts used a different look every time, so the feed didn’t read as one organization. The repositioning introduced a new logo and a bright, consistent palette and type system, and shifted the grid toward Reels featuring members.

03

Content Strategy

Weekly content creation across Instagram feed posts, Reels, and Stories. My responsibilities were graphic design, copywriting, Reels, Story creation, and content scheduling. Content was organized into three pillars:

ACultural Calendar

Posts for South Asian and general holidays.

BCommunity

Reels with the executive board and members, plus interactive Stories.

CEvent & General Meeting Promotion

Multiple posts each week, recaps, and interactive Stories.

04

Event Marketing

Every event campaign followed the same five stages:

  1. 01Campaign planning
  2. 02Content creation
  3. 03Promotion
  4. 04Event execution
  5. 05Community engagement
05

Disco Deewane

Objective: Host an off-campus event where students from across the Boston area could express themselves through music and dance.

  • Created promotional graphics and event branding
  • Developed Instagram feed posts and Story campaigns
  • Highlighted the DJ of the night and the music being played
  • Built anticipation through countdowns and reminders
  • Documented the event through Stories
06

Mock Shaadi

Objective: Host a mock South Asian–style wedding night for students of all cultures and ethnicities to enjoy and experience.

  • Created promotional graphics and event branding
  • Developed Instagram feed posts and Story campaigns
  • Highlighted the itinerary of the night and the music being played
  • Built anticipation through countdowns and reminders
  • Documented the event through Stories
