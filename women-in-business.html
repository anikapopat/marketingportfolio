Women in Business

Women in Business is a student-led organization at Suffolk University that empowers, unites, and supports women in business through resources, business education, and networking opportunities. I worked on its brand repositioning, weekly content, and event marketing.

01

Objectives

  • Establish a recognizable brand identity
  • Grow digital presence
  • Strengthen community engagement
  • Drive event and general meeting awareness and attendance
  • Position Women in Business as a leading student organization
02

Brand Repositioning

The old identity was a mix of styles from post to post, so nothing tied it together. The repositioning gave Women in Business one look: a new logo, a consistent grid, a fixed color palette, and a three-typeface system.

03

Content Strategy

Weekly content creation across Instagram and TikTok. My responsibilities were graphic design, copywriting, Reels, Story creation, and content scheduling. Content was organized into three pillars:

AProfessional Development

Career tips, mini lessons, and reposts from external sources.

BCommunity

General meeting Stories and interactive Reels and TikToks.

CEvents & General Meetings

Weekly general meeting posts, event promotion, and recaps.

04

Event Marketing

Every event campaign followed the same five stages:

  1. 01Campaign planning
  2. 02Content creation
  3. 03Promotion
  4. 04Event execution
  5. 05Community engagement
05

Women’s History Month Celebration

Objective: Create a flagship event celebrating women’s achievements while increasing Women in Business’s visibility on campus.

  • Created promotional graphics and event branding
  • Developed Instagram feed posts and Story campaigns
  • Highlighted keynote speakers and special guests
  • Built anticipation through countdowns and reminders
  • Documented the event through Stories
06

International Women’s Day Fair

Objective: Celebrate International Women’s Day with a campus-wide event that connected students with women-owned businesses, campus organizations, and resources, while increasing awareness of Women in Business.

  • Developed promotional materials
  • Positioned the fair as both a celebration and a chance to discover local businesses and organizations
  • Created event announcements, reminders, and countdown content
  • Highlighted participating vendors and organizations ahead of the event
  • Planned in-person activities for attendees
